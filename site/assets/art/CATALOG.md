# Historic posters, plates, charts and maps for the walls of Cloud Harbor Laboratory

77 real, scanned, public-domain (or CC0 / CC BY) historic items, prepared with `tools/art.py` into this folder as `p_*.jpg`
and listed in `manifest.json`; their full sources and licences are in `../CREDITS.md`. They are for furnishers to hang with
`f.framed` (see `site/src/furnish/README.md`, "Framed pictures"). Nothing here is invented or redrawn: each is a scan or
photograph of the thing itself.

How to use a row:

- **slug** is the id you give `f.framed`. Agree among yourselves who hangs what, so that the same picture does not turn up in every room.
- **px** is the JPEG's size (none is over 1200 on its longest side) and **w : h** its proportions; `f.framed`'s `w` and `tall` must be in that ratio.
- **real size** is the original's width by height. Where it begins "c." it is the usual size of that publication or format, not a
  measurement of this copy. Book plates and small diagrams are marked "hang enlarged": a laboratory would have had them as wall charts.
- **hang at** is a believable size on the wall in feet, width by height, already in the right ratio: copy it straight into
  `f.framed(wall, x, z, w, tall, slug, {...})`. Posters and maps can go a little larger on a long corridor wall; plates should not.
- **rooms** use the laboratory's own names: the Lecture Room (Emergence and Complexity), Room 102 (Statistical Physics and Phase Transitions),
  Room 103 (Theory of Computation), Room 201 (Morphogenesis and Self-Organization), Room 202 (Dynamical Systems and Chaos), Room 205 (Philosophy
  of Mind), Room 206 (Deep Learning), Room 207 (Neural Computation), the Collective Behavior Laboratory (Collective Behavior and Artificial Life),
  the Stone Library (Abstraction: from Plato's Forms to Learned Representations), the Museum Café and study room, the Stair Hall and corridors,
  faculty offices, the attic common room and Rindge Library's rooms. They are suggestions, first choice first.
- **caption** is one sentence for the card (`text:`); use the title for `title:`, the maker and date for `kicker:`, and the holding institution
  for `foot:` (it is in CREDITS.md and in the manifest's `source`).
- The WPA posters are colour silkscreens of 1936-41: pin or frame them plainly (thin dark `frame`, `mat: 0`). The plates, maps and
  engravings want a mount (`mat`) and glass. The School of Athens, the Cary map and the Chéret are the big pieces: give each a wall.
- The Russian, Latin and language pieces date from when those were taught here. They stay as general wall pieces for corridors, offices, the
  café and the library; a few (Schleicher's tree, the alphabets) also suit a course, as their rows say.
- Good pairs and sets: the four WPA month posters (January, March, September, October) down a corridor; `p_theory_of_seasons` with
  `p_earth_annual_revolution`; the two spectra; the two Bentley snow crystals; the six Haeckels (three of them radiolarians and diatoms); the three
  Hooke plates; Babbage's plan with Lovelace's table, the Jacquard loom and Jacquard's woven portrait; the Golgis and Cajals with the Vesalius
  brain and Galvani's frogs; Kepler's nested solids with Leonardo's icosahedron and Byrne's Euclid; the three Shepherd maps of the ancient world.
- `p_phrenology_symbolical_head` is there as a historical curiosity; its caption says so. Keep that sense if you rewrite it.
- `p_cajal_nerve_cells` has the weakest source of the set (see CREDITS); where one neuron drawing is wanted, prefer the two Golgi plates or `p_cajal_retina`.
