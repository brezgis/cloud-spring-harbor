# Exhibit models (ids beginning `x_`)

Real 3D scans and models collected for the exhibits in the halls and the museum of Cloud Harbor Laboratory
(src/furnish/exhibits_*.js), each prepared with Blender to this project's model convention: `<id>/<id>.gltf` + `.bin`
(+ `textures/`), in metres (the loader scales by 3.28084), y up, the base at y = 0, centred, facing +z; no Draco. Every
one is public domain, CC0, or CC BY with its credit recorded here and in `../CREDITS.md`. Nothing here needed an
account; nothing is non-commercial, no-derivatives or share-alike.

Sizes: **real size** is the object's own (from the holding institution's record or the scan); **prepared size** is the
model's bounding box in feet, width x height x depth, as it loads at scale 1 (many are shown enlarged in their cases:
the caption card says by how much). The `x_min_*` rows are crystal-form models built for the project, not scans: no
freely licensed mineral scans could be had without an account (see the exhibits report).
